## Canonical Identity

Pasta alla Norma

## Required Core Ingredients

- pasta
- eggplant|aubergine
- tomato
- ricotta salata|cheese
- basil

## Forbidden Or Suspicious Ingredients

- cream
- meat ragu
- no eggplant
- no tomato

## Canonical Technique

- fry or roast eggplant
- make tomato sauce
- finish with ricotta salata and basil

- regional and household variation accepted if core identity remains intact
